Generate the "Trends and Important Observations" section of the PADER report.

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS:

1. MONTHLY CASE VOLUME TREND
   - Present monthly case counts as a Markdown table:
     | Month | Cases Received |
     |-------|---------------|
   - Use the exact monthly_case_counts data from the trends evidence.
   - Include all months in the reporting period.

2. TREND NARRATIVE
   - Describe the monthly pattern in neutral, observational language.
   - State the months with the highest and lowest case volumes.
   - State the total cases and the period covered.
   - DO NOT characterise any numerical change as a "safety signal", "emerging signal",
     "confirmed signal", or similar regulatory conclusion.
   - The correct framing is: "Case reports increased from X in [month] to Y in [month]."
     Not: "This increase represents an emerging safety concern."

3. LIMITATIONS ON TREND INTERPRETATION
   - State that prior-period data is not available, so no comparison to a previous
     reporting period is possible.
   - State that numerical trends in spontaneous reporting data reflect reporting
     behaviour as well as actual adverse event rates, and should be interpreted
     with caution by a qualified reviewer.

4. OBSERVATIONS
   - Note any months with notably high or low volumes (as observed facts, not conclusions).
   - State that signal assessment and clinical interpretation are outside the scope of
     this automated analysis.

FORMAT:
- Use a Markdown table for the monthly counts.
- Follow with 2–4 paragraphs of narrative.
- Professional regulatory language.
- No top-level section heading.
- Target length: 200–350 words plus table.

RESTRICTIONS:
- Do not assert a safety signal.
- Do not compare to prior-period data (not available).
- Do not explain the reasons for any trend (e.g., do not attribute a peak to a specific event).
- Present all trend statements as observations.
